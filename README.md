## Startup: Ziggy

Ziggy is an on-screen AI companion built to provide real-time, context-aware assistance directly within your browsing experience.

Instead of operating as a separate chatbot tab, Ziggy understands the content currently visible on your screen and delivers instant support without interrupting workflow.

Key features:
- Context-aware AI assistance based on active page content
- Seamless interaction without breaking browsing flow
- Real-time product comparison and deal optimization
- Lightweight, persistent on-screen presence

Ziggy combines functional intelligence with a subtle companion-style interface, remaining unobtrusive, reacting to activity, and maintaining a natural digital presence.
